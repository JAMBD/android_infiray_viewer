# Privacy Policy

**Thermal Camera**

This app allows you to view and capture thermal camera footage.

## Data Collection

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All thermal images and videos are saved locally to your device's storage and never leave your device.

## Third Parties

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: Required to access the thermal camera hardware
- **Storage**: Required to save captured images and videos to your device
